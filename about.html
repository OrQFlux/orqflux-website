About OrqFlux

We believe every business process deserves its own AI agent.

OrqFlux was founded to close the gap between what AI can do and what businesses actually experience day to day. Most business interactions still run on WhatsApp chains, spreadsheets, and phone calls. We are changing that.

We started OrqFlux after watching capable businesses lose hours every day to the friction of operations, sales teams manually relaying orders over WhatsApp, finance teams assembling audit bundles by hand, operations managers chasing down tracking numbers.

The technology to fix this existed. What was missing was the right layer to connect it, a platform that could take a business process, wrap it in an intelligent agent, and make that agent reachable from whatever messaging platform the customer already uses.

That is what OrqFlux is. Not an AI chatbot. Not another CRM. A platform that turns critical business journeys into deployable, analytics-tracked AI agent journeys and gets businesses live in days, not months.

"Every business operation that involves a customer or process interaction can be AI-transformed. We are building the platform that makes that true for any business, on any stack, in any market."
