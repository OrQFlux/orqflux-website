FAQ

Everything you need to know.

An agent journey is a persistent, stateful AI workflow that handles one complete business process from start to finish. It remembers context, calls the right systems at each step, applies your business rules, and writes every event to an analytics store. Once built, it runs without human intervention unless you have defined an escalation condition.
WhatsApp Business API, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, Slack, SMS/RCS, voice channels with speech-to-text, and email. The same agent logic runs across all channels. Your customers use whichever they prefer, and OrqFlux normalises everything into a single workflow.
